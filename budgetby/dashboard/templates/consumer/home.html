How BudgetBy Protects You from Fake Discounts

Online stores frequently inflate MRPs right before a sale to make fake discounts look huge. Here is how our automated multi-window verification guarantees authenticity:

1

24/7 Automated Scraping

Our distributed crawler engines scan prices across 100,000+ products on Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, and Nykaa in 30–60 minute intervals.

2

30, 60 & 120-Day Baseline Verification

Every claimed discount is checked against 30, 60, and 120-day rolling median prices as well as historical minimum prices. If a seller bumped their price yesterday to advertise a fake 50% drop, our algorithm flags or discards it.

3

Direct Official Checkout

BudgetBy never collects payments, debit/credit cards, or passwords. Every "Get Deal" button takes you straight to the official retailer's verified checkout.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does BudgetBy verify that discounts are genuine?

We log price changes continuously. When a deal is posted, we calculate the discount against the product's 30, 60, and 120-day rolling median prices as well as its historical minimum recorded price, not just the seller's claimed MRP. If the seller raised the price just before the sale, our multi-tier baseline algorithm rejects the fake discount.

Is BudgetBy 100% free to use?

Yes. BudgetBy is completely free for shoppers. We earn a small affiliate commission from merchant partners when you complete a purchase through our links, with zero added cost to you.

Do I need to sign up or create an account to save deals?

No account is required. When you click the heart icon on any deal, it is saved securely to your browser's local storage so you can easily access your shortlist anytime without sharing any personal information.

Which stores are currently tracked on BudgetBy?

We track 5 major platforms in India: Amazon India, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, and Nykaa. We focus exclusively on verified retailers with dependable customer return policies.
