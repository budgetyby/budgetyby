Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how BudgetBy works and keeps shopping honest.

How does BudgetBy determine if a price drop is real? ▼

Many e-commerce sellers artificially inflate MRPs before applying fake discounts. BudgetBy continuously logs the actual selling price of 100,000+ products across multiple stores. When a price drops, we compare it against the product's 30-day median selling price rather than the inflated MRP.

Is BudgetBy completely free for shoppers? ▼

Yes, 100% free! You don't even need to create an account. You can search, browse price drops, and save items to your personal watchlist completely free of charge.

How do affiliate links work on BudgetBy? ▼

When you click "Check Deal" and make a purchase on Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, or Nykaa, we may receive a small referral commission from the retailer at zero additional cost to you. This supports our server costs and 24/7 price scanning crawlers.

Do I enter my payment or credit card details on BudgetBy? ▼

Never. BudgetBy is strictly a price tracker and deal discovery portal. All transactions, payments, deliveries, and customer service take place directly on the official retailer websites (e.g. Amazon.in or Flipkart.com).
