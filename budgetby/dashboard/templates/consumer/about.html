Our Deal Verification Standards

1. All-Time Lows (ATL): A product earns the 🏆 ATL badge when its current verified price equals or drops below the lowest recorded price in our database history.

2. 24-Hour Price Drops: We compare the current deal price against the exact price recorded 24 hours prior. Only items with a verified negative price delta are featured in the Drops aisle.

3. Community Re-check: Every shopper can tap Request Price Check on any card to request an immediate scraper re-evaluation if a merchant changes prices.
